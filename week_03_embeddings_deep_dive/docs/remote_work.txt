REMOTE WORK AND FLEXIBLE WORKING POLICY

Hybrid Work Model:
TechCorp supports a hybrid work arrangement that balances productivity with flexibility. Most roles follow a hybrid model with a mix of in-office and remote work.

In-Office Requirements:
- Minimum 2 days per week in the San Francisco office
- Required days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Allows flexibility for Monday and Friday remote work
- Team leads may adjust requirements based on project needs
- Enables collaboration, mentoring, and team building

Remote Work Eligibility:
- Most positions are eligible for remote work arrangements
- Eligibility determined during hiring or role change
- Some positions (security, facilities, reception) require full in-office presence
- Performance and responsibility level may influence approval

Work From Home Days:
- Monday and Friday are designated remote work days
- Flexible arrangements available with manager approval
- Remote work allows focus on deep work and individual tasks
- Employees must be responsive during standard working hours

Full Remote Options:
- Full remote work available for specific roles
- Requires manager approval and senior leadership sign-off
- Available for proven, self-motivated employees
- Time zone alignment considered for team collaboration
- Must visit office for quarterly in-person meetings

Equipment and Allowances:
- $500 one-time home office setup allowance
- Reimbursement for ergonomic chair, desk, monitor, keyboard, mouse
- Laptop and peripherals provided by company
- Monitor and docking station provided or reimbursed
- Internet connectivity allowance: $50/month reimbursement

Internet and Connectivity:
- Reliable high-speed internet required for remote work
- Company reimburses up to $50/month for internet service
- VPN required for secure access to company systems
- Clear guidelines for cybersecurity and data protection

Communication Expectations:
- Remote employees must be available during core hours (9am-3pm Pacific)
- Video enabled during meetings and standups
- Prompt responses to Slack, email, and other communication tools
- Asynchronous communication encouraged for distributed teams
- Time zone considerations for scheduling

Meeting and Collaboration:
- All meetings should have video enabled for remote participants
- Calendar should clearly indicate remote days
- Synchronous collaboration during in-office days
- Video conferencing tools (Zoom, Google Meet) are standard
- Recording of meetings for those unable to attend

Performance and Accountability:
- Performance expectations are the same for remote and in-office work
- Deliverables and deadlines apply equally
- Regular 1-on-1s with managers continue whether remote or in-office
- Progress tracked through projects, not physical presence
- Managers focus on outcomes, not visibility

Wellness While Remote:
- Mental health resources available
- Encourage breaks and movement throughout the day
- Maintain work-life boundaries
- Professional development opportunities
- Community events for remote employees

Expense Reimbursement:
- Home office equipment: up to $500
- Internet: up to $50/month
- Co-working space (if needed): by approval
- Backup internet/mobile hotspot: by reimbursement
