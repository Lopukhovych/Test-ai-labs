EMPLOYEE BENEFITS AND COMPENSATION

Health Insurance:
- Comprehensive health insurance covering medical, dental, and vision
- Medical coverage: 80% company paid, 20% employee responsibility
- Dental and vision coverage included
- Family coverage available with subsidized rates
- Mental health and wellness programs
- Annual health checkups and preventive care covered

Retirement Planning:
- 401(k) retirement savings plan with company match
- Company matches up to 4% of employee salary
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, full vesting after 4 years
- Access to financial planning resources
- Roth 401(k) option available
- Investment diversification options

Paid Time Off:
- Generous vacation policy with tiered increases
- 0-2 years employment: 15 days vacation per year
- 2-5 years employment: 20 days vacation per year
- 5+ years employment: 25 days vacation per year
- 10 paid holidays per year (New Year, Thanksgiving, Christmas, etc.)
- Sick days: 5 additional days per year for illness
- Personal days: 2 additional days per year for personal needs
- Parental leave: 12 weeks paid leave for new parents
- Sabbatical: Eligible after 5 years of service

Professional Development:
- $1000 annual learning and development budget
- Conference attendance support and reimbursement
- Online course subscriptions and certifications
- Internal training programs and workshops
- Tuition reimbursement for relevant degrees

Wellness and Lifestyle Benefits:
- Gym membership reimbursement up to $75/month
- Yoga and meditation classes on-site
- Free lunch on office days
- Subsidized cafeteria pricing
- Employee wellness challenges with prizes
- Mental health counseling services (EAP)

Additional Perks:
- Free coffee, tea, and beverages in break rooms
- Commuter benefits and parking subsidies
- Employee discount programs on tech products
- Pet-friendly office policy
- Flexible work arrangements
