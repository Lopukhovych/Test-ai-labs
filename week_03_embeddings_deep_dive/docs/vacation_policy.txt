VACATION AND TIME OFF POLICY

Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy:
TechCorp believes that work-life balance is essential for employee wellbeing and productivity. All employees receive paid vacation time based on tenure.

Vacation Day Allowances:
- Employees with 0-2 years of service: 15 vacation days per year
- Employees with 2-5 years of service: 20 vacation days per year
- Employees with 5+ years of service: 25 vacation days per year
- New hires receive prorated vacation based on start date

Vacation Request Process:
- Submit vacation requests at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal
- Requests require manager approval
- Coordinate with team to ensure adequate coverage
- Vacation requests are typically approved unless conflicts exist
- Emergency time off can be requested with supervisor notification

Carrying Over Unused Days:
- Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next calendar year
- Maximum carryover of 5 days per year
- Carryover days must be used within the first quarter of the following year
- Cash payout of unused days is available upon termination

Holiday Schedule:
- 10 paid holidays per year (New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day)
- Additional holidays may be granted for religious observances with manager approval

Sick Days:
- 5 sick days per year for illness, medical appointments, or health-related needs
- Sick days do not carry over to the next year
- Medical documentation may be required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days

Personal Days:
- 2 personal days per year for personal business
- Personal days must be requested in advance when possible
- Can be used for any reason with minimal notice if unexpected

Parental Leave:
- 12 weeks paid leave for birth parents
- 8 weeks paid leave for adoptive and non-birth parents
- Additional unpaid leave available for bonding
- Flexible return-to-work options

Sabbatical Program:
- Eligible after 5 years of continuous service
- 1 month fully paid sabbatical leave
- Additional unpaid leave available by arrangement
- Used for rest, learning, travel, or personal projects
